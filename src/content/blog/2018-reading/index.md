For anyone who’s curious, and for my own records, I suppose, the following is a list of books I read in 2018, including some rereads.

- _Euthydemus_, Plato
- _Dhammapada_, attributed to the Buddha
- _De Magistro_, Augustine
- _The Existence of God_, Richard Swinburne
- _The Miracle of Theism_, J.L. Mackie
- _Dialogues Concerning Natural Religion_, David Hume
- _Hume on Religion_, David O’Connor
- _The Antichrist_, Friedrich Nietzsche
- _Prolegomena to Any Future Metaphysics_, Immanuel Kant
- _Tractatus Theologico-Politicus_, Baruch Spinoza
- _Spinoza’s Tractatus Theologico-Politicus: The Cambridge Critical Guide_, eds. Yitzhak Y. Melamed and Michael A. Rosenthal
- _Hellenistic Philosophy_, eds. Brad Inwood and L.P. Gerson
- _Scholastic Metaphysics_, Edward Feser
- _Leviathan_, Thomas Hobbes
- _Meditations, Objections, and Replies_, Rene Descartes
- _Spinoza_, Michael Della Rocca
- _Treatise on the Emendation of the Intellect_, Baruch Spinoza
- _Ethics_, Baruch Spinoza
